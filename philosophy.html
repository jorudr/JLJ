About JORU — Private Desktop Trading Journal & Analytics

JORU (also known as J.L.JÖRMUNGANDR and JLJ) is a desktop trading journal and analytical workspace designed for traders who treat trading as an empirical discipline.

Developed by Ivan Vorobei, an independent software engineer based in Podgorica, Montenegro. JORU was built out of frustration with clunky spreadsheets and invasive cloud SaaS journals that store sensitive financial history on third-party servers.

The Offline-First Privacy Guarantee

Your trading history, sizing, and edge are confidential proprietary information. Cloud trade trackers store your executions, drawdown records, and API credentials on remote databases subject to breaches and data mining.

JORU runs 100% offline using a local embedded SQLite database on your computer. Google Sign-In is only utilized to verify account status and forum participation; your trade data never leaves your disk.
