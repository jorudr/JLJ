About JORU

JORU is a desktop trading journal and analytical workspace for reviewing decisions, tracking results and developing trading rules. It is built for traders who want to understand what drives their results rather than just log trades.

JORU is developed by Ivan Vorobei, an individual developer based in Podgorica, Montenegro. See the Terms of Use for the full legal details.

Trade data, journal entries and exchange API keys stay on your device — they are never uploaded or sold. Google Sign-In is used only to identify your account; see the Privacy Policy for details.

JORU is also known as J.L.JÖRMUNGANDR and JLJ, reflecting the project's name and visual identity.

Questions or feedback: info@gandr.site or the Telegram community. Explore the trading journal or download the application.
